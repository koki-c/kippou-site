No two stones are ever the same.
Pick up what you dig and look as long as you like.

Dig, Look, Display

All you do is tap, swipe to pick up, and turn to look.
You don’t need to know a thing about minerals. Kirara the fox will teach you.

  • No time limits
  • Close the app anytime, and it’s all still there
  • The first cave is free

Tap Crystals to Dig

Tap the crystals growing in the cave. Cracks run through them, pieces fall away, and gems come out.

Take a Closer Look

Turn each stone with your finger and look closer with a loupe. Some glow under UV light. Snap a photo of your favorites to keep.

Display Your Favorites

Display the ones you want to keep in the glass cases of your specimen room, each with its label beside it.

Every Stone
Gets Its Own Label

Each specimen you pick up comes with a label: its name, the cave and depth you found it at, the date, its size, and what makes it look the way it does.

A point without a single chip. A lump holding gold. A crystal with water sealed inside. Even among quartz, no two labels are alike.

Ordinary labels carry no scores or grades. Only the very best get a “Select” or “Museum” mark, with a line on what makes them special. Star the ones you love.

Choose the Room, Too

Each cave has its own specimen room, and you can change the whole room into whichever one you like. Try any room in your own specimen room before you buy it. You pay with the money from selling the stones you dig.

The deeper you go, the more rooms the Furnisher in town has for sale.
There are plenty more than shown here.

Cave Songs on a Music Box

The songs you heard in the caves are added to the music box one by one. Pick a favorite and play it in your specimen room.

Kirara Makes Herself at Home

Look around your specimen room and you may find her napping on a case, mumbling sleepily about her dreams of stones.

Over 20 Kinds of Gems and Minerals

Even stones of the same kind differ in shape, size, and quality. There are 7 caves. Deeper caves have different scenery, and different gems to find.

Quartz with gold, amethyst with a phantom, trapiche emerald. More than 70 kinds of specimens in all.

Every gem is modeled by comparing it with photos of real specimens.
