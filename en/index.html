Every so often, a crystal gives up something special

Tap the crystals growing in the cave to dig them out. Most of what falls is just fragments. But every so often, out comes a well-formed gem, or one with another stone trapped inside.

Crystals don’t only grow in columns. You’ll find them deep in rock crevices, in mud-filled pockets, and in clusters hanging from the ceiling.

All you do is tap, swipe to pick up, and turn to look. No mineral know-how needed.

When her hunch pays offSee? Right where it glowed.
Never misses, right?

No two gems are ever the same

Each one differs in shape, size, and depth of color. A point without a single chip. A piece holding gold. A crystal with water sealed inside.

Every gem you dig up is recorded with its name, where and when you found it, its size, and what it looks like.

Ordinary finds get no scores or grades. Only the best ones get a “Select” mark, and the rarest of all get “Museum.”

After a standout find…My tail won’t stop.
Eh. It’s a good stone.

Pick it up and look as long as you like

Turn it with your finger. Hold it up to the light. Make the right tools, and you can study it through a loupe or shine UV light on it. Find the perfect angle and take a photo to keep.

A room for the gems you dug yourself

Keep the ones you love in glass display cases.

Redo the whole room in any style you like.

There’s even a room just for your very favorites.

Kinds you haven’t found yet stay as “???” in your field guide.

There are 7 caves. The deeper you go, the more the scenery changes, and so do the gems. Over 20 kinds of gems and minerals. Count variants like quartz with trapped water or quartz with gold separately, and there are more than 80.
